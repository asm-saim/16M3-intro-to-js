# 1. Number
Types of numbers:
- Positive Number (+ve): 2, 5, 10
- Negative Number (-ve): -4, -9
- Zero: 0

# 2. Integer
Whole numbers without decimal points.

Examples:
23, 44, 2, 5

Types:
- Odd Number: 3, 5, 7
- Even Number: 2, 4, 6

# 3. Float / Decimal Number
Numbers with decimal points.

Examples:
2.3, 5.77, 9.99

# 4. Basic Operations
Addition:       +
Subtraction:    -
Multiplication: *
Division:       /

Examples:
5 + 3 = 8
10 - 4 = 6
3 * 4 = 12
20 / 5 = 4

# 5. Modulus Operator (%)
Returns the remainder after division.

Examples:
10 % 3 = 1
15 % 2 = 1
20 % 5 = 0

Common Uses:
- Check odd/even
- Cyclic operations
- Game logic

# 6. Percentage
Formula: (Part / Total) * 100

Examples:
5% of 200
= (5 / 100) * 200
= 10

10% of 500
= (10 / 100) * 500
= 50

# 7. Sum
Adding multiple numbers together.

Examples:
15 + 33 + 55 = 103

# 8. Average (Avg)
Formula:
Sum of numbers / Total numbers

Examples:
22 + 44 + 55 = 121

Average:
121 / 3 = 40.33

# 9. Maximum and Minimum
- Maximum = Biggest number
- Minimum = Smallest number

Examples:
22, 64, 232

Maximum = 232
Minimum = 22

# 10. Series
A sequence of numbers.

Examples:
1, 2, 3, 4, 5
2, 4, 6, 8, 10

Common Types:
- Increasing Series
- Decreasing Series
- Fibonacci Series

Fibonacci Example:
0, 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8

3. Square Root: √16 = 4

4. Random Number: Used in games and simulations.

5. Logic:

NOT (!)
